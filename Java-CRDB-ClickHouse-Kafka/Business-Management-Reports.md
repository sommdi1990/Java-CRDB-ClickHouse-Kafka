## هدف

گزارش‌های مدیریتی و executive reports.

## Report Types

### 1. Financial Reports

- Balance sheet
- Income statement
- Cash flow
- Financial analysis

### 2. Operational Reports

- Performance metrics
- KPI dashboards
- Operational efficiency
- Resource utilization

### 3. Business Intelligence

- Data analytics
- Trend analysis
- Predictive analytics
- Business insights

## Report Features

### Scheduling

- Daily reports
- Weekly reports
- Monthly reports
- Custom schedules

### Distribution

- Email delivery
- Dashboard display
- Export to PDF/Excel
- API access

## Integration

- **Report Manager**: برای report generation
- **Accounting Service**: برای financial reports
- **Analytics Service**: برای business intelligence
